# ASTRAL STREAM: The Frequency Chronicles
### *Master Game Design Document & Narrative Architecture*

---

## 1. Vision & Core Fantasy
*Astral Stream* is a vibrant retro 8-bit musical creature-collecting RPG. In a world where music is an invisible cosmic energy streaming through the stars, the player carries an **Astral Tuner**—a celestial frequency device capable of tuning into cosmic wavelengths and streaming living spirit animals out of the ether.

### The Core Pillars:
1. **Streaming from the Ether**: No physical balls or tapes. Creatures are summoned and collected by tuning into cosmic broadcast frequencies (FM / AM / Quantum bands).
2. **Cute & Cozy Musical Animals**: Adorable critters infused with musical instruments (Chime-Cats, Bass-Hounds, Brass-Bunnies, Cloud-Sloths).
3. **Rivals-to-Companions**: Exploring the world means discovering cool, stylish rivals, dueling them in friendly resonance tests, and recruiting them to your stream squad.
4. **Style-Warping Bosses & Cleansing Waves**: Dissonant "Dead Channels" hijack the signal, warping reality into jarring anti-styles (CRT static, monochrome ink, wireframe). Defeating them triggers a glorious, screen-filling **Cleansing Wave** that explodes with color and triumphant music!
5. **Dynamic Web Audio Engine**: Your active animal playlist literally composes the background music in real time.

## 2. The Playable Demo Level: "The Neon Shoreline & The Dead Channel"

### Demo Story Arc (15–20 Minute Focused Experience):
* **Act 1: Awakening on Frequency Beach**
  * You wake up on the colorful pastel sands of the Neon Shoreline. Your Astral Tuner chimes to life.
  * *Tuning Tutorial*: You align your frequency dial to **98.0 FM** to stream your starter companion: **Chime-Cat** (The 8-Bit Synth Kitten).
* **Act 2: The Static Incursion (The Warping)**
  * Suddenly, the sky flickers. Colors drain into buzzing grayscale TV static and rolling VHS scanlines. The music muffles and crackles with white noise.
  * **Dead Channel 000: The Signal Overlord** has jammed the shoreline.
* **Act 3: Rival Encounter — Jax the Shadow Punk**
  * You meet **Jax**, a cool, brooding punk bassist holding an electric spiked bass alongside his loyal **Bass-Hound** (88.3 FM).
  * Jax challenges you to a friendly resonance battle to test if you're strong enough to face the anomaly. Beat him, and he joins your party!
* **Act 4: The Anomaly Showdown & Dual-Stream Mashup**
  * You and Jax breach the center of the static storm.
  * You activate the **Dual-Frequency Mashup**, fusing *Chime-Cat* + *Bass-Hound* into the colossal **Cyber-Fuzz Chimera**!
  * You defeat the Signal Overlord.
* **Act 5: The Cleansing Wave Celebration**
  * The screen shatters in a blinding burst of rainbow light.
  * The audio filter snaps open with a triumphant, foot-tapping chiptune-rock drop!
  * Palm trees bloom in neon pink and mint green, wild animals dance, and Jax high-fives you as the demo concludes.

## 4. Boss Specification: Dead Channel 000 (The Signal Overlord)
* **Visual Style**: Glitchy CRT monitor head, television static scanlines, chromatic aberration, flickering frames.
* **Audio Distortion**: Bitcrush audio processing, wow/flutter pitch-bending, white noise bursts.
* **Battle Attacks**: *Static Burst*, *Channel Desync*, *Low-Pass Muffle*.
* **Cleansing Payoff**: Explodes into thousands of rainbow pixel sparks; restores 60 FPS vibrant neon lighting and full-spectrum stereo audio.
