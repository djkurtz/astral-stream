# ASTRAL STREAM: The Frequency Chronicles
### *Master Game Design Document (Modern Streaming & Audio-Match Edition)*

---

## 1. Vision & Core Fantasy
*Astral Stream* is a vibrant, colorful retro-modern musical creature-collecting RPG. In a world where music streams through the cosmos like high-speed data, players carry a sleek **Vibe-Phone** capable of scanning ambient soundwaves, matching audio profiles, and streaming living spirit animals directly into their living playlist.

### The Modern Core Pillars:
1. **Audio Match Radar (The Shazam / Sound-ID Mechanic)**: No physical balls, tapes, or dusty radio dials. Players hold up their device, pulse a sonic radar into the air to listen to the ambient beat, and sync with the waveform to stream the creature into their library.
2. **Cute & Cozy Musical Animals**: Adorable critters infused with modern musical genres and instruments (Chime-Cats, Bass-Hounds, Brass-Bunnies, Cloud-Sloths).
3. **Rivals-to-Companions via "Playlist Blend"**: Meeting cool rivals leads to friendly battles. Defeating them lets you initiate a **Collaborative Blend Session** (like Spotify Blend), merging your tracks into a shared mashup!
4. **Style-Warping Bosses & Cleansing Drops**: Dissonant "Dead Channels" and Glitch Signals corrupt reality into jarring anti-styles (CRT static, monochrome ink, wireframe). Defeating them triggers a glorious, screen-filling **Cleansing Drop** that explodes with color and triumphant music!
5. **DJ Launchpad Battle HUD**: Turn-based tactical battles where attacks are triggered via tactile, glowing beat-pads (Stem Pads) that light up to the rhythm.

## 2. The Playable Demo Level: "The Neon Shoreline & The Dead Channel"

### Demo Story Arc:
* **Act 1: The Sound Scan on Frequency Beach**
  * You wake up on the colorful pastel sands of the Neon Shoreline. Your Vibe-Phone detects an unidentified audio stream.
  * *Audio-Match Tutorial*: You pulse your sonic radar ring, tap to the rhythm, and match the signature to stream your starter companion: **Chime-Cat** (`#ChiptuneSynth`).
* **Act 2: The Glitch Stream Incursion (The Warping)**
  * Suddenly, an unverified pirate broadcast hacks the feed. Colors drain into buzzing grayscale TV static and rolling scanlines. The music muffles and crackles with white noise.
  * **Dead Channel 000: The Signal Overlord** has hijacked the shoreline.
* **Act 3: Rival Encounter — Jax the Shadow Punk**
  * You meet **Jax**, a brooding punk bassist with an electric spiked bass and his loyal **Bass-Hound** (`#SpikedBass`).
  * Jax challenges you to a friendly battle to test if your audio setup is strong enough. Beat him, and you link devices to form a **Collaborative Blend**!
* **Act 4: The Boss Anomaly & The Playlist Blend**
  * You and Jax breach the center of the static storm.
  * You activate the **PLAYLIST BLEND MASHUP**, cross-streaming *Chime-Cat* + *Bass-Hound* into the colossal **Cyber-Fuzz Chimera**!
  * You defeat the Signal Overlord.
* **Act 5: The Cleansing Drop Celebration**
  * The screen shatters in a blinding burst of rainbow light.
  * The audio filter snaps open with a triumphant, foot-tapping chiptune-rock drop!
  * Palm trees bloom in neon pink and mint green, wild animals dance, and Jax high-fives you as the demo concludes.

## 4. Boss Specification: Dead Channel 000 (The Signal Overlord)
* **Visual Style**: Glitchy CRT monitor head, television static scanlines, chromatic aberration, flickering frames.
* **Audio Distortion**: Bitcrush audio processing, wow/flutter pitch-bending, white noise bursts.
* **Battle Attacks**: *Static Snow Burst*, *Stream Desync*, *Low-Pass Muffle*.
* **Cleansing Payoff**: Explodes into thousands of rainbow pixel sparks; restores 60 FPS vibrant neon lighting and full-spectrum stereo audio.
