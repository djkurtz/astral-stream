IMPERIAL FIELD MANUAL & OBJECTIVES

🌟 Empire Goals

Your objective is to expand from your home world (Nova Terra) into a thriving interplanetary civilization. Extract raw minerals from planets and asteroids, smelt them into resilient alloys, research advanced astrophysics, build starfleets, and defend against lawless pirate havens.

⚡ Resource Economy

  • ⚡ Energy: Powers your planetary buildings and shields. If energy is negative, mining and smelting efficiency drops.
  • ⛏️ Minerals: Basic building block mined from planetary crust and harvested from asteroid belts.
  • ⚙️ Alloys: High-grade space alloys refined by Foundries. Essential for constructing starships and new colony bases.
  • 🔬 Science: Knowledge produced by Astro-Physics Labs to discover advanced blueprints and technologies.

🚀 Fleet Classes

  • Vanguard Scout: Fast reconnaissance ship to explore hostile sectors.
  • Prospector Harvester: Automated mining drone. Deploy to the Cerberus Asteroid Belt for constant mineral extraction.
  • Aegis Corvette & Titan Frigate: Combat warships equipped with plasma cannons and point-defense systems.

🎮 Navigation & Controls

Pan: Click and drag anywhere in the space viewport.
Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel or pinch trackpad.
Select: Click directly on any star, planet, moon, or station to inspect it in the right command panel.
