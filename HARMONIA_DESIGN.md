# Harmonia: Opus of the Ensemble 🎼🐾
*A Musical Monster-Tamer RPG of Practice, Repertoire, and Orchestral Mastery*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
In the enchanted realm of **Sonora**, music is not merely an art—it is the lifeblood of nature and soul. Every sentient creature's musical devotion manifests as a sentient, adorable companion known as a **Harmonipet**. A violinist's passion takes form as a graceful *Swan*, a trumpeter's boldness as a jaunty *Terrier*, a drummer's steady pulse as an energetic *Raccoon*, and a flutist's breath as a singing *Finch*.

Players embark on an epic quest starting as a humble novice soloist. By practicing their craft, discovering and deciphering historic sheet music, challenging fellow musicians in musical jam battles to recruit them, and organizing diverse instrument sections, the player expands their ensemble from **Solo $\rightarrow$ Duet $\rightarrow$ Trio $\rightarrow$ Quartet $\rightarrow$ Chamber Band $\rightarrow$ Grand Symphony Orchestra**.

---

## 2. Core Game Pillars

### 🎻 A. The 4 Instrument Families & Harmonipet Sections
Musicians and their pets are categorized into four distinct acoustic sections:
1. **Strings Section (Harmony & Agility)**
   - *Instruments*: Violin, Viola, Cello, Double Bass, Harp, Acoustic Guitar.
   - *Pet Archetypes*: Swans, Foxes, Hares, Fawns, Cats.
   - *Musical Affinity*: Rich counterpoint, lyrical vibrato, resonant sustain.
2. **Woodwinds Section (Flow & Melodic Precision)**
   - *Instruments*: Concert Flute, Piccolo, Oboe, Clarinet, Saxophone, Bassoon.
   - *Pet Archetypes*: Finches, Otters, Owls, Frogs, Badgers.
   - *Musical Affinity*: Rapid staccato runs, melodic embellishment, dynamic breath control.
3. **Brass Section (Power & Projection)**
   - *Instruments*: Trumpet, French Horn, Trombone, Euphonium, Tuba.
   - *Pet Archetypes*: Terriers/Hounds, Rams, Lions, Elephants, Boars.
   - *Musical Affinity*: Fanfares, fortissimo swells, brassy harmonics that cut through noise.
4. **Percussion Section (Drive & Tempo Stabilization)**
   - *Instruments*: Snare Drum, Bass Drum, Timpani, Marimba, Glockenspiel, Taiko.
   - *Pet Archetypes*: Raccoons, Gorillas, Squirrels, Bears, Beavers.
   - *Musical Affinity*: Metronomic precision, polyrhythms, syncopated accents that shield against tempo wobble.

---

### 🎹 B. Musicianship Mastery & The Practice Shed (Grind Mechanics)
True musicianship is earned through practice. The player can enter the **Practice Shed** at any rest point or music studio to hone their skills through interactive practice drills:
- **Metronome Rhythm Drill**: Hit notes in sync with accelerating or syncopated BPM beats to boost **Tempo Stability**.
- **Scale & Arpeggio Run Drill**: Match pitch sequences across the circle of fifths to increase **Technique**.
- **Tone & Dynamics Shaping**: Modulate volume (Pianissimo to Fortissimo) smoothly to elevate **Tone Quality**.
- **Sight-Reading Challenge**: Quickly transcribe musical prompts to sharpen **Sight-Reading**, which drastically reduces the practice time needed to master new sheet music.

#### Musicianship Attributes:
- **Technique (TEC)**: Determines accuracy, crit harmony chance, and execution speed.
- **Tone Quality (TON)**: Boosts audience engagement, resonance power, and harmony damage.
- **Tempo Stability (TEM)**: Prevents tempo rush/drag under pressure and shields against enemy dissonance.
- **Sight-Reading (RDG)**: Accelerates learning new repertoire pieces from raw sheet music.

### 📜 C. Repertoire & Sheet Music System
Throughout the towns, conservatories, and wild shrines of Sonora, players discover **Sheet Music**.
- Each piece is a real playable procedural musical composition!
- Pieces require specific **Instrumentation & Section Formations**:
  - *Solo*: "Minuet in G" (Solo Violin or Solo Flute)
  - *Duet*: "Two-Part Invention" (1 String + 1 Woodwind)
  - *Trio*: "Bossa Nova Serenade" (1 Guitar/String + 1 Sax/Wind + 1 Snare/Percussion)
  - *Quartet*: "String Quartet No. 1" (2 Violins, 1 Viola, 1 Cello) or "Wind Quintet in E-flat"
  - *Chamber / Jazz Combo*: 6–8 varied instruments.
  - *Grand Orchestral Masterwork*: Full 12–16 piece ensemble with balanced String, Wind, Brass, and Percussion sections.
- Mastering a piece unlocks it for **Concert Performances** and unlocks powerful **Ensemble Harmony Moves** during battles.

---

### ⚔️ D. Musical Audition Battles (NPC Recruitment)
When exploring town plazas, riverside pavilions, and mountain amphitheatres, players meet NPC musicians playing their instruments.
- To convince an NPC to join your ensemble, initiate a **Musical Audition / Jam Battle**!
- In battle, the player and their current ensemble perform musical motifs:
  - *Melodic Call & Response*: Counter the opponent's melody with harmonizing intervals (Thirds, Fifths, Octaves).
  - *Section Synergies & Counters*:
    - Percussion anchors and counters fluttering Woodwinds.
    - Woodwinds weave around and soften aggressive Brass.
    - Brass overpowers and supports delicate Strings.
    - Strings blend and enrich Percussion rhythm.
- Filling the **Audition Harmony Meter** to 100% earns the musician's deep respect, prompting them and their Harmonipet to join your Ensemble Roster!

---

### 🏆 E. Ensemble Evolution & Conservatory Competitions
As your ensemble grows, your status in Sonora elevates:
- **Tier 1: Street Soloist** (Local coffee shops, busking corners)
- **Tier 2: Harmonious Duet** (Salon recitals, local music fairs)
- **Tier 3: Vibrant Trio** (Town amphitheaters, festival stages)
- **Tier 4: Masterful Quartet / Quintet** (Regional conservatories, guild halls)
- **Tier 5: Chamber Orchestra & Big Band** (Grand Opera Houses)
- **Tier 6: Royal Philharmonic Orchestra** (The Grand Symphony Hall of Sonora)

At each tier, player ensembles enter **Competitive Music Showdowns** against rival ensembles (e.g. *The Allegro Duo*, *The Starlight Brass Quintet*, *The Maestros of Sonora*). Performing pieces with high technical precision, dynamic flair, and synchronization earns **Reputation Stars**, prize money, rare instruments, and access to the next realm!

## 3. Visual & Audio Aesthetic
- **Visuals**: Crisp, top-down 2D pixel art with vibrant palettes, expressive musicians carrying their instruments, and lively pets walking beside them or perching on shoulders.
- **Audio Engine**: 100% procedural WebAudio synthesis featuring multi-voice physical modeling (karplus-strong for strings, filtered FM/pulse for woodwinds and brass, noise/sub envelopes for percussion) providing rich polyphonic orchestration that dynamically layers as ensemble members join!
