# Astra Imperium: Master Design Document & Architectural Archive

## Vision & Core Thesis: "The Weight of the Scepter"
*Astra Imperium* is a multi-layer empire builder where the player starts as a nameless scavenger in the rust dunes of Nova Terra and climbs the societal hierarchy across 6 distinct tiers to become the Supreme Galactic Emperor. As the player's physical footprint expands across the stars, their direct human connection transitions from intimate camaraderie to cold, abstracted macro-strategy.

---

## The 6-Tier Progression Ladder

### Tier 1: Rogue Scavenger (Personal Survival & Hand Tools)
* **Scope**: Personal Survival, Scrap Sorting, Tool Maintenance, Kinetic Resonance.
* **Locations (Central Viewport)**:
  1. *The Scrap Dunes*: Active prospecting of salvage nodes, dodging acid dust storms.
  2. *The Makeshift Shelter*: Workbench, tool crafting, manual hand-crank dynamo.
  3. *Foreman Briggs' Exchange Depot*: Scrap turn-in, bounty contracts, avoiding predatory shakedowns.
  4. *The Rusty Valve Cantina*: Madame Nyx black market booth, drifter rumors, helper scouting.
* **Active Toy**: Precision Scrap Scanning & Rhythmic Dynamo Resonance (audio-visual ratchet and lightning arcs).
* **Friction / Risk**: Tool durability degradation, heat buildup, Briggs' extortionate quotas.
* **Transition Gate**: Assemble Workshop Assembly Rig ($1,200\text{ Scrap}$, $450\text{ Energy}$, $150\text{ Alloys}$) and purchase Workshop Charter.

### Tier 2: Work Crew Boss (Workshop & Labor Gangs)
* **Scope**: Direct Helper Management, Workstation Adjacency Combos, Wage Inflation.
* **Locations**:
  1. *The Workshop Floor*: Assigning procedural helpers (*Laborer, Technician, Smelter, Tinkerer*) to machine bays (*Shredder, Blast Furnace, Battery Array*).
  2. *The Foreman's Office*: Payroll management, grievance settlement, Unit 73 analytics.
  3. *The Labor Hall*: Recruitment depot; inspecting procedural candidates with trait dualities (*Overclocker, Union Loyal, Sycophant, Slacker*).
  4. *High-Altitude Loading Docks*: Cargo freighter logistics, negotiating corporate contracts with Baroness Vane.
* **Active Toy**: Workstation Adjacency Combos & The Glowing "Overdrive" Emergency Lever.
* **Friction / Risk**: Dynamic wage inflation ($W \propto N^{1.15}$), worker stress, strikes, machine blowouts.
* **Transition Gate**: Incorporate Industrial Syndicate ($5,000\text{ Min}$, $2,500\text{ Alloy}$, $1,000\text{ Sci}$) with 8-man crew at $>85\%$ morale.

### Tier 3: Industrial Syndicate Baron (District Enterprise & Submanagers)
* **Scope**: Executive Delegation, City Infrastructure Power Grids, Corporate Sabotage.
* **Locations**:
  1. *Apex Executive Spire*: Penthouse overlooking metropolis; directing 3 Submanagers (*Director Valerius [Ops], Quartermaster Tanya [Logistics], Dr. Thorne [R&D]*).
  2. *Automated Megafoundry Complex*: Robotic assembly bays fabricating heavy machinery.
  3. *Under-City Shadow Exchange*: High-tech black market for AI cores and corporate espionage.
  4. *The Gilded Gantry Club*: Elite lounge for political dealmaking and contract auctions.
* **Active Toy**: Substation Power Grid Routing & Executive Submanager Talent Trees.
* **Friction / Risk**: Grid transformer overloads, pollution fines, corporate saboteurs and blackouts.
* **Transition Gate**: Build Orbital Spire & Space Elevator ($15,000\text{ Alloy}$, $10,000\text{ Min}$, $5,000\text{ Sci}$).

### Tier 4: Planetary Governor (Global Policy & Sovereign Diplomacy)
* **Scope**: Global Legislation, Faction Diplomacy, Planetary Crisis Management.
* **Locations**:
  1. *High Senate Chambers*: Holographic council table with the 4 alien race leaders (*Lithoid, Cybernetic, Solaris, Sylva*).
  2. *Global Logistics Command*: Holographic planetary projection managing energy grids and trade corridors.
  3. *Sovereign Planetary Archives*: Lost colonization history and ancient terraforming protocols.
  4. *Undercity Resistance Quarter*: Smog slums where Briggs leads a populist insurgency.
* **Active Toy**: Faction Summits & Holographic Edict Battles (passing global laws with multi-faction leverage).
* **Friction / Risk**: Ideological unrest, alien civil wars, planetary crises (*Seismic Rupture, Bio-Spore Blight*).
* **Transition Gate**: Ratify Planetary Unification Accord ($100\%$ Hegemony) and launch Orbital Space Program.

### Tier 5: High Chancellor of Helios (Solar Hegemony & Interplanetary Fleets)
* **Scope**: Multi-World Colonization (*Nova Terra, Ares, Cerberus Belt, Zeus*), Fleet Command.
* **Locations**:
  1. *Solar Command Flagship Bridge*: Tactical star charts, fleet telemetry, orbital convoy dispatch.
  2. *Helios Dyson Orbital Drydock*: Solar-harvesting rings assembling capital warships.
  3. *Ares Frontier Citadel*: Red-dust military bastion and terraforming control center.
  4. *Madame Nyx's Eclipse Station*: Hollowed-out asteroid in Kuiper belt for deep-space black-ops.
* **Active Toy**: 3D Solar System Fleet Maneuvers, Rock-Paper-Scissors Naval Battles, Convoy Escort Defense.
* **Friction / Risk**: Pirate Armadas (*Skull Rock*), interplanetary transit distance lag, fuel starvation.
* **Transition Gate**: Eradicate Skull Rock Pirate Fortress and build Interplanetary High Command Citadel.

### Tier 6: Galactic Emperor (Cosmic Vision & Megastructures)
* **Scope**: Cosmic Ascension Doctrines, Dyson Swarms, Megastructure Engineering.
* **Locations**:
  1. *The Throne of Starlight*: Floating dais suspended at the galactic nexus.
  2. *The Hyperlane Gate Core*: Ancient megastructure connecting the star system to the wider Milky Way.
  3. *The A.R.I.A. Transcendence Vault*: Quantum intelligence matrix running planetary calculations.
  4. *The Cosmic Horizon*: Observation deck peering into the deep extragalactic void.
* **Active Toy**: Phased Megastructure Construction & Imperial Ascension Doctrines.
* **Friction / Risk**: Extragalactic Swarm Incursions, Hyperlane Collapse Anomalies.
* **Transition Gate**: Initiate the Great Cosmic Convergence (Final Victory).

## Anchor Characters & The Trait Matrix

### Anchor Characters
1. **Unit 73 $\rightarrow$ A.R.I.A.**: Companion robot that evolves from a dented scrap-bot into an omniscient imperial AI. Features the "memory purge" moral dilemma and the "glitch aesthetic" callback in Tier 6.
2. **Foreman Briggs**: The dark mirror of the player. Rises from Scrapyard Tyrant $\rightarrow$ Rival Workshop Boss $\rightarrow$ Corporate Fixer $\rightarrow$ Rebel Leader $\rightarrow$ Pirate Warlord $\rightarrow$ Broken prisoner before the Starlight Throne.
3. **Madame Nyx**: The enigmatic shadow broker offering dangerous technological shortcuts; revealed in Tier 6 as an emissary of an ancient precursor civilization.

### Trait Dualities for Procedural Personnel
* `[⚡ Overclocker]`: $+50\%$ workstation speed, $+100\%$ tool wear and double energy drain.
* `[☕ Union Loyal]`: $+25\%$ output when crew morale $>80\%$, triggers collective strikes if wages drop.
* `[🤫 Sycophant]`: $+10\%$ boost to adjacent workers, produces zero base output.
* `[💤 Slacker]`: $30\%$ lower wage, $20\%$ chance to fall asleep (requires click to wake).
* `[🧠 Tinkerer]`: Randomly generates Science while working, occasionally dismantles equipment.
